Who We Are

HISUN Motors USA is the exclusive distributor of HISUN powersports vehicles in the United States. Headquartered in McKinney, Texas, our team focuses on delivering versatile, feature-rich vehicles designed for real-world use — from property management and outdoor work to trail riding and everyday recreation.We bring together product direction, customer insight, and market expertise to deliver vehicles that combine durability, comfort, and modern functionality — all at an exceptional overall value.

Stryker

Engineered for Impact, Designed for Control
Built for those who demand both performance and practicality, the Stryker 750X brings premium features to the trail. Equipped with electric power steering (EPS) and front and rear cameras for precision and safety, it offers seamless control in any environment. Its vented windshield, rear poly windshield, half poly doors, and LED lighting package ensure protection and superior visibility. The advanced 10-inch color touch screens with Bluetooth, Apple CarPlay, FM/AM radio, and integrated speakers keep you connected, while the addition of a rear bumper and step rails enhances both utility and style.

Stryker Crew

Bring the Whole Crew
The all-new Stryker 750X Crew delivers unmatched power, cutting-edge technology, and rugged durability, all while comfortably seating six passengers. Designed to conquer tough terrain, it features Electric Power Steering (EPS) for precise control and dual 10-inch color LCD screens with Apple CarPlay, Bluetooth, FM/AM radio, and integrated door speakers, keeping you connected and entertained on every ride. Front and rear cameras enhance visibility, while a push-button start adds convenience. Built to tackle any challenge, it comes standard with a front windshield, 3500 lb winch, and heavy-duty side rails for added protection.
Learn More

Guardian

Ready to Hit Every Trail
The Guardian series offers options for a rugged 2-up ATV built for two riders, featuring a comfortable backrest, heated hand grips, and a heated seat for added comfort on any ride. It includes a 7" full-color TFT digital display with rider info, altitude gyroscope, and tire pressure sensor, along with selectable 2WD/4WD and front and rear locking differentials for superior control. Equipped with a 2,500 lb. winch, front and rear racks, LED high/low beam headlights, and turn signals, the Guardian 750L is ready for any terrain and condition.
